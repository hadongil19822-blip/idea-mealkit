# IDEA MEALKIT graphic identity

The complete IDEA MEALKIT wordmark anchors the opening screen. Three oversized graphic ingredients make the identity tangible: a disc that becomes a diamond, four accordion strips, and a diagonally divided square. Their typography cycles IDEA / MEAL / KIT into THINK / MAKE / PLAY and back, with a pause between transformations. Type and surfaces have separate motion tracks so words stay upright and readable.

Use warm black and paper white, with muted sage confined to one ingredient. Desktop uses a horizontal composition; mobile stacks the two compact shapes over the wide word strip. The complete brand name remains fixed and readable above the changing type. On scroll the composition expands into the existing project reveal. Keep native sticky scrolling, the pause control, and a static reduced-motion composition.

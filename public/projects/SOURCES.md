# Project cover provenance

All projects are explicitly selected by the site owner. No assets from the visual design reference are reused.

- `metalook.jpg`: screenshot of the owner's live https://metalook.store, captured through the browser.
- `semapage.jpg`: screenshot of https://semapage.ai.kr/, the production address confirmed in the owner's `sema-page` repository README; captured 2026-10-09.
- `brandeye.jpg`: screenshot of https://brandeye.ai.kr/, the production domain confirmed by the owner. Captured through the browser on 2026-10-09; replaces the older Scope service screenshot.
- `/HangulWave_icon_512.png`, `/metalook.png`: existing project logos in this repository.
- `cosmic-icon.png`: the owner's official app icon from `hadongil19822-blip/cosmic-spell-survivors-support/assets/app-icon.png` on GitHub, read through the GitHub connector.

- `cosmic-missiles.webp`, `cosmic-boss.webp`: the owner's screenshots from the public App Store page https://apps.apple.com/us/app/cosmic-spell-survivors/id6815463401, opened through the authenticated App Store Connect app's “App Store에서 보기” control. Apple ID 6815463401 and the matching public product title were verified on 2026-10-09.
- `hangul-home.webp`, `hangul-choice.webp`: the owner's screenshots from https://apps.apple.com/us/app/hangulwaves/id6767775835, linked from the same developer's public App Store page. Matching product and developer verified on 2026-10-09.

The four WebP files were acquired through the browser pageAssets capability, without URL guessing or image alteration. The monochrome treatment is applied in CSS; actual service screenshots retain their original colors.

Descriptions and addresses for Sema Page, BrandEye and Cosmic Spell Survivors were checked against the owner's repositories and public pages. HangulWave and Cosmic Spell Survivors now link to their verified public App Store product pages.

## Generated editorial assets — 2026-10-09

The owner authorized generated imagery to replace the repeated model photograph. `editorial/botanical.jpg`, `editorial/fashion.jpg`, `editorial/textile.jpg`, and `editorial/sculpture.jpg` were created with the built-in image_gen tool. They are editorial concept assets. Prompts and placement are documented in `editorial/PROMPTS.md`. Existing product screenshots and verified store links remain as the actual product previews.

## Color variants and Mealkit identity — 2026-10-09

`editorial/botanical-color.jpg` and `editorial/sculpture-color.jpg` were edited from the previously generated photographs using the built-in image_gen tool. Their source images remain unchanged. Full edit prompts are in `editorial/COLOR-PROMPTS.md`. These are editorial concept images, separate from the actual product screenshots.

The live page uses the original generated monochrome botanical and sculpture assets again. Color variants remain available but unused.
